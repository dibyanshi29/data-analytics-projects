# Data Analytics Projects

Two hands-on projects by **Dibyanshi Poddar** · [Portfolio](https://dibyanshi29.github.io) · All data is fictional.

| Project | What it shows | Tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**E-commerce SQL Case Study**](sql-ecommerce-case-study/) | 15 business questions on 27,960 orders: RFM segmentation, cohort retention, Pareto analysis, courier performance, coupon effectiveness | SQL (SQLite): CTEs, window functions, joins |
| [**Food Delivery Demand Forecast**](python-demand-forecast/) | Forecasts daily orders (9.1% average error) and turns the forecast into a delivery-partner staffing plan | Python: pandas, scikit-learn, matplotlib |

## Highlights

- The top 20% of customers bring in **71.8% of revenue**, and first-month retention fell from **71% to 26%** for newer customers.
- One courier is on time only **58%** of the time (32% in sale weeks), against about 88% for the others.
- The forecasting model is about **a third more accurate** than a "same as last week" baseline, and the forecast becomes a staffing plan: **5 partners** at the weekday dinner peak, **6** at weekends.
